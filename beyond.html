BEYOND MATHEMATICS

A little of life
outside mathematics.

Mathematics occupies a large part of my life, but certainly not all of it. Outside research, I enjoy books, music, recitation, writing, conversation, and the many little experiences that make life interesting.

01 · Music

Music

Music has been part of my life since I was very young. My mother introduced me to music, and at the age of four I began playing my first instrument, the harmonium. I started formal training when I was six and went on to learn both Hindustani Classical Music and Rabindrasangeet for almost ten years.

Although the formal training eventually came to an end, my love for music certainly did not. The patterns, intrinsic rhythms, and beauty of Indian ragas have remained a constant part of my life. Rabindrasangeet, in particular, has always felt like a kind of medicine to me.

Musical background: Hindustani Classical Music · Rabindrasangeet · Harmonium
02 · Recitation & Performance

Recitation & Performance

Recitation has been one of the earliest and most enduring forms of expression in my life. I made my first stage appearance at just two and a half, in a local recitation competition. My parents noticed that I had a natural inclination towards recitation and storytelling and decided to introduce me to professional training. I fell in love with it almost immediately, and that love has stayed with me ever since.

At the age of eight, after an audition, I was invited to perform as a child artist for All India Radio. During my school years, I participated in inter-school recitation competitions, performed at almost every school event, and gradually found myself anchoring and hosting many of them as well.

I later received formal training in recitation under Bangiya Sangeet Parishad. The course involved seven years of rigorous training, culminating in the title of Visharad in Recitation, which I was fortunate to earn. Even today, recitation remains one of the things that gives me immense pleasure.

Recitation: Visharad · State & National-level competitions · AIR Child Recitation Artist
03 · Words, Stories & Books

Words, Stories & Books

Books have always been my best friends. Whenever I feel low, I reach for a book and curl up on my couch. Reading has always been my comfort zone, and if you ever want to give me a gift, a book is probably the easiest choice.

My love for reading naturally grew into a love for writing. I usually write in Bengali, my mother tongue, and writing has remained a quiet part of my life ever since. I still write whenever I feel overwhelmed by the thoughts in my head — putting them into words somehow makes them lighter.

One of my most memorable experiences with writing came when I won the national essay-writing competition organised by Tata Building India on “Eradication of Poverty” in Bengali and was felicitated by the then President of India, Pranab Mukherjee, in 2014.

I continue to write today. Sometimes it is poetry, sometimes prose, and sometimes simply a page full of thoughts. It is one of the simplest ways I know to quieten my mind.

04 · Speaking & Debate

Speaking & Debate

What I love most about spontaneous debate is its rawness. There is no time to decorate an argument with carefully selected quotations or neatly arranged facts. You have to speak from what you genuinely think at that particular moment. For me, that is what makes debate so interesting: it helps me form a clearer viewpoint on a subject, but it also helps me understand myself a little better.

During school, we had a house system with four houses, and I was the captain of Laxmibai House, named after the Rani of Jhansi. I regularly represented my house in inter-house debate competitions.

Public speaking has been another constant in my life. Throughout school, I was closely involved in organising events and frequently anchored cultural programmes. I still enjoy hosting and speaking in front of an audience. Public speaking gives my thoughts a channel — a way to take something that exists inside my head and share it with a much larger audience.

Debate: District-level competitions · Inter-house debates
