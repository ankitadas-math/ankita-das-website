Beyond Mathematics

A little of life outside mathematics.

Mathematics occupies a large part of my life, but it is far from being the only thing that defines me. Music, recitation, books, writing, debate and public speaking have accompanied me since childhood and continue to remain an important part of who I am.

01 · Music

Music has been an important part of my life since childhood. I began playing the harmonium at four and received formal training in Hindustani Classical Music and Rabindrasangeet from the age of six for nearly a decade.

I have participated in and won several district- and state-level competitions and continue to enjoy learning and listening to music, particularly semi-classical, light music, and classical instruments.

Singing at the Teachers' Day celebration · SRM University–AP · 2026
02 · Recitation & Performance

Recitation has been a passion since childhood, beginning with my first stage performance at two and a half. I was selected as a child recitation artist for All India Radio at eight.

Later I completed a seven-year professional course under Bangiya Sangeet Parishad, earning the title of *Visharad in Recitation*. I have participated in numerous school and inter-school performances and continue to enjoy recitation as a way of expressing words, rhythm, and emotion.

03 · Words, Stories & Books

Books have been a constant presence in my life since childhood. I love reading widely, particularly Bengali literature, and find books a source of comfort and companionship.

I also enjoy writing poetry and stories, primarily in Bengali, as a way of expressing and organizing my thoughts.

04 · Speaking & Debate

Debating has been a meaningful part of my public-speaking journey, teaching me to think spontaneously, listen to different perspectives, and articulate my ideas clearly. As a school house captain, I regularly participated in inter-house debates and helped organize events and cultural programmes. I continue to enjoy hosting events and connecting with audiences through public speaking.

05 · Life Beyond Academia

There are many things that do not fit neatly into an academic CV but make life richer. I enjoy observing people — the way they speak, sit, interact and express themselves. This habit of observation is closely connected to my love for visualising stories, people and situations.

This space is intentionally open. It will gradually become a small visual collection of the people, places, books, performances and moments that make up life beyond research.

More photographs and stories will find their place here over time.
