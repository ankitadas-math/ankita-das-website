About me

I am a PhD researcher in Mathematics at SRM University AP. My research interests lie broadly in algebraic number theory, algebraic geometry, and Diophantine equations.

My current work centres on superelliptic curves, their Jacobian varieties, and arithmetic questions concerning torsion and solvability.
