Generalized Fruit Diophantine Equation and Super Elliptic Curves

Ankita Das, Kalyan Banerjee, and Kalyan Chakraborty

Published in Monatshefte für Mathematik.

This work studies a family of Diophantine equations through the arithmetic and geometry of associated superelliptic curves, with particular emphasis on Jacobians, torsion, reduction, and Nagell–Lutz-type phenomena.
